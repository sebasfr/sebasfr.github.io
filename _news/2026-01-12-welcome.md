I am happy to announce the launch of my personal website! This platform will serve as my personal academic hub.

The site is organized into three main sections:
* **[About](/):** An overview of my research agenda.

* **[CV](/cv/):** A current curriculum vitae detailing my coursework, research assistantships, and teaching experience.

* **[Research](/research/):** A consolidated archive of my working papers, non-academic writings and fact-checking articles.

I built this site using the [al-folio](https://github.com/alshedivat/al-folio) Jekyll template. I will be updating the website regularly.
