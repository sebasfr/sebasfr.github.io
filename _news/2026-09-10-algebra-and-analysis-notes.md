Two further sets of personal class notes from the University of Costa Rica are now
available under [Notes](/notes/):

* **[Metric Spaces and Measure Theory (MA-0505)](/notes/ma0505/)** — *Análisis I*,
  taught by Dr. Pedro Méndez Hernández. Twenty chapters: metric space topology,
  compactness, completeness, the Arzelà–Ascoli theorem, connectedness and the Baire
  category theorems, followed by functions of bounded variation, the
  Riemann–Stieltjes integral, Lebesgue measure and measurable functions, the
  theorems of Egorov and Lusin, the Lebesgue integral and its convergence theorems,
  and Lebesgue's characterisation of Riemann integrability.

* **[Groups and Rings (MA-0561)](/notes/ma0561/)** — *Grupos y Anillos*, taught by
  Dr. Samaria Montenegro Guzmán. Twenty-four chapters: subgroups, cyclic groups,
  permutation groups, cosets and quotient groups, the isomorphism theorems, group
  actions and the Sylow theorems, then rings and fields, the polynomial ring,
  ideals both maximal and prime, quotient rings, ring homomorphisms, characteristic
  and the Frobenius map, the field of fractions, and the factorization of
  homomorphisms.

These were written in Spanish and translated into English. Both versions are
available for every course in the section — including the two published in April —
and you can switch between them from any page. As before, they are shared as-is,
may contain errors, and are not affiliated with the instructors.
