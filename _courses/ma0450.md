A multivariable course taught at UCR as *Cálculo en varias variables*, extending
the rigorous real-analysis treatment of single-variable calculus from $\mathbb{R}$
to $\mathbb{R}^n$. The notes follow Dr. Juan Gabriel Calvo Alpízar's lectures
and cover the topology of $\mathbb{R}^n$ (norms, open and closed sets,
compactness, connectedness), continuity and limits of functions of several
variables, differentiation (partial derivatives, the total derivative, the
chain rule, the inverse and implicit function theorems, Taylor's theorem in
several variables), multiple integration (Jordan measurability, Fubini's
theorem, change of variables) and an introduction to vector calculus.

These are personal notes I took as a student. The notes themselves are in
Spanish; this page is in English. They are shared as-is, may contain errors,
and are not affiliated with the instructor.
