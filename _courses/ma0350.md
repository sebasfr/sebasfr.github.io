An advanced single-variable course taught at UCR as *Cálculo en una Variable 2*,
giving a rigorous, real-analysis-style treatment of the calculus of real-valued
functions of one real variable. The notes follow Dr. Pedro Méndez Hernández's
lectures and cover sequences and series of real numbers, the Riemann integral,
improper integrals, and sequences and series of functions. Topics include the
convergence of sequences and Cauchy criteria, $\limsup$ and $\liminf$,
convergence tests for numerical series, the construction and properties of the
Riemann integral and its applications, improper integrals, pointwise and
uniform convergence of function sequences, power series, and Taylor expansions.

These are personal notes I took as a student. The notes themselves are in
Spanish; this page is in English. They are shared as-is, may contain errors,
and are not affiliated with the instructor.
