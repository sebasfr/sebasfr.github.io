I recently graduated with a B.A. (Honors) in Economics from the University of Costa Rica (GPA 9.6/10), where I am also enrolled in advanced courses covering measure theory, functional analysis and abstract algebra, for the a B.A. in Mathematics. I am deeply interested in **Quantitative Economics, Macroeconomics and Monetary Economics**. I currently work as a research assistant mentored by Dr. Jose P. Vasquez and Dr. Isabela Manelici (LSE) on the *Responsible Sourcing? Evidence from Costa Rica* project, now revise-and-resubmit at the *American Economic Review*, and as a Lecturer at the UCR Department of Economics, teaching introductory economics courses.

I have accepted an offer to join the [**RoMe Master in Economics**](https://www.romemaster.it/) in September 2026, a two-year graduate program jointly run by **EIEF (Einaudi Institute for Economics and Finance)** and **LUISS Guido Carli University** in Rome. The program is known for its small cohorts, intense tutoring, and a curriculum built around advanced quantitative methods applied to policy-relevant questions.

My current research utilizes structural modeling and time-series techniques to analyze inflation dynamics and monetary policy in small open economies. Driven by a desire to push the boundaries of these analytical frameworks, I am currently building a rigorous foundation in advanced mathematics. Ultimately, my goal in quantitative economics is to shift from applied analysis to methodological development, creating the mathematical tools necessary to structurally estimate novel and increasingly complex macroeconomic models.

I was awarded first place in the 2024 Latin American Reserve Fund (FLAR) Research Contest. My winning paper, *"Supply Shocks, Monetary Policy and Foreign Investment: The Costa Rican Case"*, investigates the country's atypical display of accelerated growth and deflation following global supply shocks. I used a New Keynesian Growth Model to argue that the interplay between foreign investment attraction and nearshoring was a key driver of this outcome.
